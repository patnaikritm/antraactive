Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Antra Active (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you visit www.antraactive.com or join our mailing list, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your first name (optional) and email address, only if you enter them in our mailing-list box.
  • Basic technical data that your browser shares automatically when you visit any website, such as your IP address, browser type and device. We do not currently run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

We use your email address only to send you news about Antra Active, such as our launch, early access and offers. We do not sell your information or rent it to anyone.

Who handles your data

We use third-party services to run the site and mailing list, for example website hosting, Google Sheets or another form and email-marketing provider, and Google Fonts, which loads the typefaces on this site. These providers process data on our behalf and have their own privacy policies. When you load the page, Google Fonts may receive your IP address.

Unsubscribing

You can stop receiving emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us at the address below.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in Europe or CCPA in California), you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it deleted. To make a request, contact us below and we will respond promptly.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy
